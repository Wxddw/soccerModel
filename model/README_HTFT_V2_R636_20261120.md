## Decision / production freeze
M79.3.6 Production remains frozen. ## What changed compared to existing R6.3.13 baseline
Old \`halftime_bridge_r6313.py\`: given *one full prematch* Q(h,a), split its h/a goals via separate binomial fractions \`f_home,f_away\` and collect nine HTFT classes. Fractions only; no explicit empirical HT state-transition correction.
New \`htft_transition_v2_r636.py\`: for *each* possible fulltime score (h,a), generate all feasible half-time scores (i,j) and compute:

\`P(i,j | h,a) ∝ Bin(h,i; f_home) Bin(a,j; f_away) exp(theta[WDL(i,j)+WDL(h,a)])\`

Normalize **within each fixed (h,a)**, then multiply by original \`Q(h,a)\` and sum by nine HTFT labels. Thus:
- \`sum(HTFT)=1\`; \`sum_HT P(HT,FT=c) = sum_{h,a in FT=c} Q(h,a)\`, to numerical tolerance.
- **Original FT WDL / JC / total goals / exact score Q are never altered.**
- \`theta\` is an empirical adjustment for HT lead, HT draw, HT deficit and their observed FT class; NOT a genuine minute-by-minute hazard engine or confirmation of any specific substitution.
- All nine outputs, Top1, Top2, Top3 remain Shadow, with \`formal_grant: null\`.
- Missing Q or incomplete mass, non-integer/out-of-range scores, post-cutoff source, training after source observation, missing historical result timestamps or postmatch fields in Q => fail closed.
- Training uses only prior completed matches with documented result-availability times: \`fit_logits\` fits nine ridge-penalized conditional logits. The ridge=15 and [-2,2] clip are initial research safety parameters, **not historically optimized**. Fractions are input separately and must themselves be fitted on past data.
- \`evaluate\` is strictly postmatch and cannot be used in \`predict\`.

Real external timestamp provenance must be checked separately; a self-declared JSON field is not external certification.

Result compares **two HTFT models on the exact same pre-match Q and matched fixtures**:
1. Baseline: development-fitted \`f_home,f_away\`, no HTFT logits.
2. Challenger: same Q, same fractions, nine ridge-fitted HTFT logit adjustments.

Per-match paired metrics: nine-class Log Loss, Brier, Top1/Top2/Top3 and seeded bootstrap 95% CIs on the paired differences. Also report empirical class frequencies and test coverage.
If Q is not the real frozen M79 Q, the benchmark cannot certify M79 Production. If a chronological comparison does not beat both Log Loss and Brier and maintain robust performance in multiple leagues/time regimes, remain Shadow; no automatic promotion regardless.

## Evidence and limitations
Prior R6.3.14 independent simplified-goal EPL 2025/26 holdout (380 matches):
- R6.3.13 HTFT baseline Top3 243/380, logloss 1.9276419578.
- Historical-half-fractions candidate Top3 246/380, logloss 1.9257077101.
Small benefit, mixed uncertainty, **not** this new HTFT v2 result and **not** M79-certified.

Research references:
- Maia et al., *Stochastic modelling of football matches*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04338 : time-varying goal intensities and score-state effects.
- Zou et al., *A Bayesian In-Play Prediction Model for Association Football Outcomes*, https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/10/8/2904 : in-game information and goal-time dynamics. **In-play data from these studies cannot be used as prematch observed features.**

Flaim Fantasy covers ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper fantasy leagues (American football/baseball/basketball/hockey), not historical 90-minute soccer halftime/fulltime calibration data; deliberately not called as a data source.
